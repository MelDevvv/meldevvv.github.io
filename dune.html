PROJECT 02 // GAME PROTOTYPE

DUNE

UNITY • C# • PROCEDURAL GAMEPLAY

A procedural hill-climbing prototype inspired by the core gameplay of the Dune mobile game. The player rolls up and down an evolving landscape, attempting to travel as far as possible, while the project expands the formula with a darker atmosphere, horror elements and an emerging narrative.

EXPLORE THE PROJECT ↓ ← PORTFOLIO
01 // CONCEPT

THE GAME

Dune is a gameplay prototype based around a simple but repeatable idea: keep moving, control the terrain and survive for as long as possible. The core concept takes inspiration from the Dune mobile game, where the player rolls up and down hills while attempting to travel as far as possible. I used that foundation as a starting point, then developed the idea into something darker. The prototype introduces a more unsettling atmosphere and story-driven direction, turning what would normally be a simple endless hill-climbing loop into the foundation for a larger horror experience.

ENGINE Unity
LANGUAGE C#
CORE IDEA Hill Climbing
DESIGN Procedural + Horror
02 // GAMEPLAY

CORE SYSTEMS

01

ROLLING MOVEMENT

The player controls a continuously moving character rolling across the terrain, with the shape and angle of the landscape directly influencing movement.

02

PROCEDURAL TERRAIN

The environment is designed around procedurally generated terrain, allowing the playable landscape to continue beyond a single fixed level layout.

03

DISTANCE-BASED GAMEPLAY

The central challenge is simple: continue travelling and push the player's distance further while maintaining control.

04

HILLS & MOMENTUM

Terrain shape creates changes in movement and momentum, making the physical relationship between the player and the environment an important part of the gameplay.

05

HORROR DIRECTION

The project moves beyond a straightforward arcade concept by introducing darker visual and narrative ideas to create a more unsettling experience.

06

STORY ELEMENTS

The procedural gameplay acts as the foundation for a wider story concept, giving the player a reason to continue beyond simply chasing a high score.

03 // GAME LOOP

THE LOOP

The prototype is built around a deliberately simple gameplay loop which can be expanded with additional systems, events and narrative content.

01 / MOVE Control the rolling character.
02 / SURVIVE React to changing terrain.
03 / DESCEND Use momentum to continue.
04 / PUSH FURTHER Travel further and discover more.
