01 // PROJECT OVERVIEW

EXPLORE.
INTERACT. BUILD.

Dune began as a gameplay prototype focused on exploration and interaction within a desert-inspired environment.

Dune is a Unity and C# prototype designed around the idea of giving the player a playable environment to explore while experimenting with gameplay mechanics and interaction.

A major focus of the project was creating an environment that feels like a space rather than simply a collection of objects. Level layout, player movement and environmental presentation were considered together during development.

The project also provided an opportunity to develop my understanding of gameplay programming, level design and interactive systems while taking an idea from an initial concept into a playable prototype.

03 // ENVIRONMENT
THE ENVIRONMENT MATTERS.

Dune places the player inside a desert-inspired playable environment. The level was created as part of the gameplay experience rather than simply acting as a visual backdrop.

Player movement and exploration were considered when building the environment, giving the player space to navigate through the level and discover different areas.

This helped me explore the relationship between level design, player movement and gameplay mechanics, while developing the prototype in Unity.
